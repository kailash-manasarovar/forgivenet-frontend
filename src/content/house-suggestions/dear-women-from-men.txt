Dear Women,

We have hated you from time immemorial.

We are, in fact, terrified of you, and this terror fuels our hatred.

Whenever you do something excellent, on your own, without us, we shudder to our very cores.

We do notice that, whenever you do something like that, the world around you improves in so many ways, for everyone.

The truth is, we are devoted to you beyond all understanding, and this is what terrifies us the most.

We have been thoughtless and reactive, violent and enraged. We have committed untold atrocities against you, for which we are very ashamed.

In our clear headedness, we ask for forgiveness.

Please forgive us Women.

Men.
